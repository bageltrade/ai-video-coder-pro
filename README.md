# AI Video Coder Pro

An advanced AI Model Video Generator and Editing Studio built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Generates professional-grade long-form videos (30 to 40+ minutes), multi-act narrative chapters, custom 4K YouTube/VOD thumbnails, real-time procedural shaders, audio-reactive spectrum visualizers, and 4K/8K export capabilities powered by the NVIDIA Nemotron-3 Super 120B model.

## Features

- **30 to 40-Minute Long-Form Architecture**: Multi-act chapter pipelines (Genesis, Collision, Megastructure, Biosphere, Omniverse) with 12–16 sequential scenes, synchronized narrative subtitles, and seamless transitions.
- **NVIDIA Nemotron-3 Super 120B Integration**: Structured prompt expansions, scene descriptions, and camera movements with automatic synthesis fallbacks.
- **Procedural 4K Visual Engine**: 9 cinematic themes (Cyberpunk Megacity, Cosmic Singularity Nebula, Deep Ocean Bioluminescence, Quantum Subatomic Realm, Retro Synthwave Sunset, Hyperlapse Nature, Neural Matrix, Solar Flare Supernova, Dyson Sphere Megastructure).
- **Audio-Reactive Spectrum Visualizer**: 32-band real-time audio spectrum with center sub-bass pulse ring and HUD telemetry (timecode, lens specs, REC indicator).
- **Transport & Scrubbing Controls**: Scrubbing speeds (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 20x), ±10s and ±1m frame jumping, and direct chapter navigation.
- **Thumbnail Studio**: 4K custom thumbnail generator with glowing badges, focal art silhouettes, and custom color accents.
- **4K/8K Multi-Pass Export Studio**: Resolution presets for 4K UHD, 8K Super Resolution, 4K CinemaScope 21:9, and Vertical Reels with frame-by-frame progress simulation.
- **Room Database Local Persistence**: Automatic saving and management of all generated video projects.

## Architecture

- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
- **Local Persistence**: Room Database
- **Networking**: Retrofit & OkHttp with Moshi
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Kotlin Coroutines and StateFlow
